OCR Best Practices
  • Clear Alignment: Scanned tables with sharp horizontal and vertical lines yield the highest cell detection accuracy.
  • Readable Codes: Use standard subject codes (e.g., CS501) and faculty abbreviations/IDs for automatic database mapping.
  • Editable Review: Nothing is published automatically! You will be able to review, adjust, and correct every cell in an interactive table before publication.
  • Safety Check: The system automatically verifies room availability and professor schedules to prevent double bookings.
